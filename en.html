About

Your own vocabulary deck, built in seconds

Flipword helps you save new words you encounter day to day and review them regularly. Just type a word — AI handles the rest.

01

AI-generated flashcards

Type an English word. AI fills in the meaning, pronunciation, part of speech, and an example sentence. You can edit it afterward.

02

Flip to learn

The front shows the word, the back shows the meaning and example. Tap to flip, swipe to move to the next card.

03

6 quiz modes

Choose the meaning, choose the word, fill in the blank, listen and choose, match pairs, or type the word. Practice the way you remember best.

04

Day-by-day review

Words are grouped by the day you added them, so you can review exactly what you learned yesterday or last week.

05

Learning streaks

Track your consecutive learning days and the words you've mastered, to keep a daily learning habit.

06

Sign in with Google

One tap to get started. Your cards are saved to your account and sync when you switch devices.
